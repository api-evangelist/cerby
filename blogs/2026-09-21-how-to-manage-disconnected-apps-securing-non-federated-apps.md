Disconnected applications are one of the biggest blind spots in enterprise security. These are apps that can’t easily plug into your organization’s identity management systems because they don’t support federated authentication like SAML or OIDC and may lack SCIM or APIs. The risk shows up across four key areas: credential management, authentication, identity automation, and logging and auditability.
